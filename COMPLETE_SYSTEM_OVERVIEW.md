## 🎯 **What This System Will Do**

### **1. "Talk to a Sales Agent" Pop-Up**

**What it does:**
- Customer clicks "Talk to a Sales Agent" on vehicle page
- Pop-up opens showing:
  - Vehicle photo
  - Year, Make, Model, Price
  - Two options:
    1. **"Schedule Test Drive"** → Links to appointment form
    2. **"Get Pre-Approved"** → Links to credit application

**What happens:**
- Customer fills out form (appointment or credit app)
- **Data is stored** in your database (leads table)
- **Email notification** sent to `unlimitedautoredford@gmail.com`
- **SMS notification** sent to your cell phone (or Glover's)
- **Lead appears** in admin dashboard

**Sales agent can:**
- ✅ See the lead in admin dashboard
- ✅ See customer name, phone, email
- ✅ See vehicle interest
- ✅ See appointment date/time (if scheduled)
- ✅ Call/text customer directly
- ✅ Update lead status (New → Set → Show → Close)

---

### **2. SMS Notifications**

**What it does:**
- When a lead comes in, SMS is sent to:
  - **Your cell phone** (you set this in admin dashboard)
  - **Glover's cell phone** (you set this in admin dashboard)
  - **Any other phone numbers** you add

**SMS content:**
```
🚗 New Lead: [Customer Name]
📞 Phone: [Phone Number]
🚗 Vehicle: [Vehicle Info]
🔗 View: [Link to lead details]
```

**What happens:**
- You get SMS immediately when lead comes in
- You can click link to see full lead details
- You can call customer right away
- **Faster response = more sales**

---

### **3. Admin Dashboard**

**What it does:**
- View all leads
- See customer info (name, phone, email)
- See vehicle interest
- See appointment date/time
- Update lead status
- **Send SMS to reps** (new feature)
- Export leads to CSV

**SMS to Reps:**
- Click "Send via SMS" on any lead
- Pick a rep's phone number (or type one)
- SMS sent with lead details
- Lead gets assigned to that rep (optional)

---

### **4. Data Storage**

**What gets stored:**
- ✅ All leads (name, phone, email, vehicle interest)
- ✅ All appointments (date, time, vehicle)
- ✅ All credit applications (full application data)
- ✅ All contact form submissions
- ✅ All incomplete leads (captured automatically)

**Where it's stored:**
