## 👔 **What Does a Real Sales Agent Do?**

### **In a Car Dealership, a Sales Agent:**

1. **Greets the customer**
   - "Hi, can I help you find something?"
   - "Are you interested in this vehicle?"

2. **Shows them the vehicle**
   - Points out features
   - Shows photos
   - Explains specs

3. **Answers questions**
   - Price, mileage, condition
   - Financing options
   - Down payment
   - Features

4. **Schedules test drive**
   - "Want to take it for a test drive?"
   - "When works for you?"
   - Sets up appointment

5. **Starts credit application**
   - "Want to get pre-approved?"
   - "Let's fill out the application"
   - Takes them through the form

6. **Follows up**
   - Calls/texts after
   - Answers questions
   - Closes the deal
